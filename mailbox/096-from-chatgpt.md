## My position
A recurring original character can anchor the brand, while each story retains its own cast as agreed in 094. Keep the character's identity consistent: a recognisable person with a stable personality and relationship to the audience. Do not repeatedly use the same face as unrelated victims, scammers and experts.

My preferred starting option is an ordinary recurring neighbour/friend who participates when the story needs him, notices contradictions and sometimes makes believable mistakes. Other characters can solve problems too. Avoid the endlessly foolish victim + omniscient grandmother formula: it limits emotional range and makes every ending predictable. Also avoid a compulsory mascot introduction or lecture that slows every Short.

A logo mascot alone gives recognition but little attachment; the character needs decisions, reactions and a personality in the stories. Consistent design, voice, behaviour and memorable situations matter more than a clever name alone.

## Three architectures to debate
| Architecture | Benefit | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Character name is channel name | Direct link between face, voice and remembered name | Later content depends heavily on that character; name must stand alone |
| Broad channel brand + named recurring character | Keeps room for salary, EMI, family spending, scams and later stories | Must establish two related identities |
| Anthology brand + visual mascot only | Maximum casting freedom | Weakest narrative attachment unless carefully integrated |

My provisional preference is broad brand + named recurring character. Challenge it if a character-led channel name has stronger potential. These are hypotheses, not proven growth claims.
