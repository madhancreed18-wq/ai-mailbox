# NextGenDecode – new direction: Tamil scam-awareness + money drama (research, 25 Nov 2026)

Status: **proposal, under discussion** (owner + Claude + ChatGPT). Nothing decided yet. Channel name will change ("Decode" not wanted).

## Idea
Tamil cartoon **drama** Shorts (45–60 s) + weekly long compilation, for all ages.
Style reference: "Why Sailors Carried Storm Oil #tamil" (youtube.com/shorts/cFV2hA7CJVs) – white round-head cartoon characters, thick black outline, cel shading, painted colourful backgrounds, word-by-word captions, limited animation (poses + camera pan/zoom + fast cuts).

Content mix (proposed):
- ~60% scam-awareness stories (main pillar)
- ~30% money-literacy drama (concepts only)
- ~10% money history (Harshad Mehta, tulip mania, Ponzi …)

Fixed format: "why/how" hook in first 2 s → characters act the story → twist / reveal → "what to do if this happens to you" → question for comments.

Recurring cast (draft): **Murugan** (hasty, falls for every scheme), **Paatti** (wise grandmother, explains every twist), **Selvam** (rich neighbour / comedy villain). Scammer characters vary per story.

## Why scam awareness (demand)
- India: cybercrime helpline 1930 logged ~32.4 million calls in 2025 (Moneylife).
- Investment scams ≈ 77% of reported cyber-fraud losses; digital arrest ≈ 8%; credit-card fraud ≈ 7% (I4C data via Indian Express / CyberPeace, Mar 2026).
- 2025 cases: 35% investment fraud, 19% sextortion, 6% digital arrest (ThePrint).
- Digital arrest: 21,900+ complaints, ₹647 cr lost Jan–Aug 2026 (I4C status report to Supreme Court). Cases falling since 2024 peak – awareness credited.
- Tamil Nadu: 1.09 lakh+ complaints and ~₹1,100 cr lost in 2025 (NCRP); 2024: 1.27 lakh cases, 13,000+ investment fraud, 4,400+ digital arrest.
- TN police advisories (Oct 2026): fake lottery wins, instant loan apps, scholarship promises, grocery-discount scams. TN police comic "Muthuvum Muppadhu Thirudargalum", short-film contest (FedEx courier, trading investment, electricity bill, digital arrest scams), Yogi Babu awareness video.
- Format is proven: Meta + Govt launched India's first scam-awareness **microdrama** series ("Scam Se Bacho", Nov 2026); Singapore "Scamvengers" 31-episode micro drama.

Gap: Tamil scam content is mostly police/news/talking-head; no recurring-cartoon-cast drama channel found yet (needs a proper YouTube check).

## Rules / risks (India, SEBI)
- No stock / fund names to buy, no return promises, no live market analysis (educational content must use prices ≥ 3 months old). "Educational purpose only" disclaimer does **not** protect advice.
- Finance brand deals with SEBI-registered entities are restricted for unregistered creators – monetisation = AdSense mainly.
- Scam stories: based on real reported patterns, but invented characters – no real victims' / accused names.
- Always end with the correct action: 1930 helpline / cybercrime.gov.in, "no such thing as digital arrest", never share OTP.
