# Asset sheet rules (needed for clean tracing)

- Pure white background, no texture, no floor shadow that touches another cell.
- Bold black outline, flat colours, light grey shading only (no gradients, no glow).
- Grid layout with wide space between items; label in bold caps BELOW each item, never touching it.
- Characters: same size and same head size in every cell, full body, feet visible, facing 3/4 to the right.
- Props/equipment on their own (no people) unless the request says "action pose".
- No real brand logos or product names on equipment.
- PNG, 1536×1024 or larger.

## Base style text (start every prompt with this)
Colourful whiteboard cartoon style, round white head with simple big eyes, white cartoon gloves, bold black outline, flat colours, light grey shading, pure white background, grid layout with wide spacing, label in bold caps under each item, no overlapping, same scale in every cell.
