Conveyor full speed-la forward odudhu. Stop press panni udane Reverse press pannaa, gearbox enna aagum?

Car-la full speed-la reverse gear podamaattom. Mudhalla nikkuvom. Motor-um apdi dhaan. Stop pannaalum rotor innum suthum. Udane reverse-naa current spike, gearbox-ku adi.

Adhanaala PLC-la oru timer. Motor OFF aanadhum anju second count aagum. Adhu mudiyura varaikkum, Reverse press pannaalum start aagaadhu.
