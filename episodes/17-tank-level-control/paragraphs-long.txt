Mudhalla correct program paarppom. X0 low sensor, X1 high sensor. Rendum water thottaa ON aagum. Pump rung-la X0 NC contact, adhukku parallel-aa Y0 holding contact. Adhukku pinnaadi X1 NC contact. Kadaisila Y0 pump coil. Low-la start, high-la stop.

Program-la adhigam varra mistake onnu. Sensor contact type-ai kozhappikkuradhu. Low sensor-na, low aanaa ON aagum-nu ninaichu X0 NO contact poduvaanga. Aanaa float, water thottaa dhaan ON. Tank full-aa irukkumbodhu X0 ON, NO close, pump odum. Tank kaali aanaa X0 OFF, pump nikkum. Ellaame thalaikeezha. Program ezhudhura munnaadi, sensor endha nilaiyila ON-nu field-la check pannunga.
