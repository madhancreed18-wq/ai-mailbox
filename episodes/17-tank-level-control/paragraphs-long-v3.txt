Oru factory-la cooling water tank. Tank-la oru float switch. Water kurainjaa pump ON, niraincha pump OFF. Simple. Aanaa pump oru rendu second ON, rendu second OFF-nu aadikittae irukku. Oru vaarathula contactor contact kariyaagi, pump motor sooda aagi trip aagudhu.

Float switch nalla dhaan irukku. Wiring-um correct. Appo thappu enga?

Veetla overhead tank-ai yosinga. Thanni kammi aanadhum motor podureenga. Tank full aagura varaikkum motor odum. Paadhi tank aanadhum off panna maatteenga. Full aanadhum dhaan off. Thirumba konjam kammi aanadhum udane podra maatteenga. Kaali aagura nilaikku vandhaa dhaan thirumba podureenga.

Factory tank-la oru float switch mattum irundhaa, adhukku andha arivu illa. Water level andha oru point-ai thottadhum OFF. Pump nikka, konjam water use aaga, level konjam keezha irangudhu. Udane ON. Thanni alaiyaa aadinaale ON, OFF. Adhu dhaan aattam.

Idhai PLC-la sariyaa pannuvom. Mudhalla inputs, outputs. X0, tank low level sensor. X1, tank high level sensor. X2, keezha sump-la water irukkaa-nu paakkura dry run sensor. X3, tank mela overflow sensor. Outputs, Y0 pump contactor, Y1 alarm lamp. Ovvoru sensor-um water thottaa ON aagum.

Ippo ladder. Mudhal rung, pump rung. Left power rail-la irundhu aarambikkurom. Mudhalla X0 NC contact. Water low sensor-ku keezha irundhaa X0 OFF, adhanaala NC contact close. Adhaavadhu, tank kaali aanaa dhaan indha vazhi thirakkum.

Adhukku pinnaadi series-la X1 NC contact. Water high sensor-ai thottaa X1 ON aagum, NC contact open aagum, pump nikkum. Adhukku pinnaadi X2 NO contact. Sump-la water irundhaa mattum close. Adhukku pinnaadi X3 NC contact, overflow vandhaa open. Kadaisila Y0 coil. Right power rail.

Ippo mukkiyamaana vishayam. X0 NC contact-ku parallel-aa, oru Y0 NO contact podurom. Idhukku holding contact-nu peyar. Y0 coil ON aanadhum, indha contact-um close aagum.

Rendaavadhu rung, alarm rung. X3 NO contact, adhukku pinnaadi Y1 alarm lamp coil. Tank overflow level-ai thottaa alarm eriyum. Pump rung-la irukkura X3 NC contact pump-aiyum nirutthidum.

Ippo oru quiz. Oruthar holding Y0 contact-ai X0-ku parallel-la podaama, thappaa X1-ku parallel-la pottuttaaru. Enna nadakkum? A, pump stop aagaama tank overflow aagum. B, pump low level kitta ON, OFF-nu aadum. C, onnum maaradhu, normal-aa velai seyyum. Yosichu vechukonga.

Factory-la nadakkura oru mistake. X2 dry run sensor-ai podama vittuduvaanga. Sump-la water illa, aanaa tank low-la irukku. Pump start aagum. Thanni illama pump odum. Seal eriyum, pump podhum. X2 NO contact rung-la irundhaa, sump kaali aanadhum vazhi udayum, pump nikkum.

Innoru mistake. High level sensor-la paasi pidichu, X1 signal varave varaadhu. X1 NC contact close-aave irukkum, pump nikkaadhu. Appo dhaan X3 overflow sensor kaappaathum. Pump-ai nirutthi, alarm-um eriyum.

Summary paarppom. Inputs, X0 low, X1 high, X2 dry run, X3 overflow. Pump rung, X0 NC, X1 NC, X2 NO, X3 NC, Y0 coil, X0-ku parallel-aa Y0 holding. Alarm rung, X3 NO, Y1 lamp.

Ippo andha quiz-ku varuvom. Answer B. Mudhalla correct ladder-la power epdi oodudhu-nu paarppom. Tank kaali. X0 NC close, X1 NC close, X2 close, X3 close. Y0 ON, pump odum, holding Y0 contact close aagum.

Water low sensor-ai thaandudhu. X0 ON aagi, NC contact open aagudhu. Aanaa power, parallel-la irukkura Y0 holding vazhiyaa pogudhu. Pump odikittae irukkum. Water high-ai thodudhu. X1 NC open. Rung udayum, Y0 OFF, holding-um open. Pump nikkum. Water keezha irangi high-ai vittu poyidum, X1 close aagum. Aanaa X0 innum open, holding-um open. Pump odaadhu. Low-ku keezha pona dhaan thirumba start.

Holding-ai X1-ku parallel-la pottaa? X0 NC series-la thaniyaa nikkum, adhukku bypass illa. Water low-ai thaandina udane X0 open, pump stop. Konjam water use aanaa thirumba ON. Low level kitta pump aadum. Adhaan answer B.

Low-kum high-kum naduvula irukkura indha idaivelikku dead band-nu peyar. Holding contact dhaan indha dead band-ai kudukkudhu. Idaiveli periya-dhaa irundhaa, pump start aagura dhadavai kurayum. Contactor-um motor-um romba naal varum.
