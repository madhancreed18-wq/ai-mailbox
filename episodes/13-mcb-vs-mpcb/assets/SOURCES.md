Narration accuracy note: magnetic trip is model-specific. The cited GV2P14 example specifies a fixed 138 A magnetic threshold; it does not establish a universal threshold of thirteen times the adjustable overload setting. Do not animate that ratio as universal. No audio was edited for this delivery.

The reference-sheet PNGs use generic redrawn contours and verified dimensions, rather than reproducing branded manufacturer sheets. Use the linked originals when modelling exact OEM details. In particular, contact geometry and the phase-loss mechanism need an OEM section drawing if a physically exact reconstruction is required.
