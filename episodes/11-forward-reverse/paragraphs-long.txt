Power wiring. Mudhalla MPCB. Adhulendhu L1, L2, L3, forward contactor KM onnu-oda top terminal-ku pogudhu. Adhe L1, L2, L3, reverse contactor KM rendu-oda top terminal-kum pogudhu. Rendu contactor-kum input orey maadhiri.

Ippo output side. KM onnu bottom terminal, T1, T2, T3, nerdiyaa overload relay-ku pogudhu. Overload-lendhu motor U, V, W. Idhu forward. Normal order.

KM rendu bottom terminal-la dhaan trick. T2 nerdiyaa KM onnu T2-ku link. Aana T1-ai KM onnu T3-kum, T3-ai KM onnu T1-kum cross pannuvom. Appo KM rendu close aanaa, L1 W-ku pogum, L3 U-ku pogum. Motor reverse.

Ippo control wiring. PLC inputs. Forward push button, NO, X zero-ku. Reverse push button, NO, X one-ku. Stop push button, X two-ku. Overload relay contact, ninety five, ninety six, X three-ku.

Stop button-um overload contact-um NC-aa wire pannuvom. Wire cut aanaalum, contact loose aanaalum, input OFF aagi motor nikkanum. Idhu fail-safe. Adhanaala ladder-la Stop-um overload-um NO contact-aa varum.

PLC outputs. Y zero, KM onnu coil-ku. Y one, KM rendu coil-ku. Aana nerdiyaa illa. Y zero-lendhu, KM rendu-oda auxiliary NC contact vazhiyaa, KM onnu coil-ku pogum. Y one-lendhu, KM onnu-oda auxiliary NC contact vazhiyaa, KM rendu coil-ku pogum.

Idhu dhaan hardware interlock. KM onnu close-aa irundhaa, adhoda NC contact open. PLC Y one ON sonnaalum, KM rendu coil-ku supply pogaadhu. Contact weld aagi stuck aanaalum, idhu kaappaathum.

Rung rendu, Reverse. X one-ku parallel-aa Y one contact. Series-la Stop, overload. Appuram Y zero-oda NC contact. Kadaisila Y one coil.
