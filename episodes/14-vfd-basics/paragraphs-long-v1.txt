Appo motor speed-ai edhu decide pannudhu? Voltage illa. Frequency.

Motor speed formula romba simple. Speed sama, nooru-irubadhu into frequency, divided by poles. Namma supply fifty hertz. Naalu pole motor-na, nooru-irubadhu into fifty, divided by naalu, sama aayirathu-ainnooru RPM. Load-oda konjam slip irukkum, adhanaala nameplate-la aayirathu-naanooru-naarpadhu RPM-nu irukkum.

Quick question. Idhey naalu pole motor-ku, irupathi-anju hertz kudutha, speed evlo? A, aayirathu-ainnooru. B, ezhunooru-aimbadhu. C, moovaayiram. Yosichu vechukonga, konja neram kazhichu answer paakkalaam.

Supply frequency-ai namma maatha mudiyaadhu. EB fifty hertz dhaan kudukkum. Adhanaala, motor-ku munnaadi oru device venum. Fifty hertz-ai eduthu, namakku venumgira frequency-la pudhu supply ready pannanum. Adhu dhaan VFD. Variable Frequency Drive.

VFD-ai open panni paarthaa, ulla moonu main stage irukku.

Mudhal stage, rectifier. Moonu phase AC-ai diodes vechu DC-aa maathudhu. Ippo frequency-ye illa. Fifty hertz-um poiduchu.

Rendavadhu stage, DC bus. Periya capacitors. Rectifier kudukkura DC-la ripple irukkum. Capacitor adha smooth pannudhu. Four-fifteen volt supply-ku, DC bus kitta thatta ainnooru-arubadhu volt irukkum.

Moonaavadhu stage, inverter. IGBT-nu solra fast switches. Ivanga oru second-ku aayira kanakkula ON, OFF aagudhu. Ovvoru pulse-oda width-ai maathi maathi kudutha, motor-ku adhu sine wave maadhiri theriyum. Idhu dhaan PWM.

Switching vegatha maathinaa, sine wave-oda frequency maarudhu. Irupathi-anju hertz venumna, slow-aa maathum. Fifty hertz venumna, fast-aa maathum. Adhanaala dhaan VFD-la speed eppadi venumnaalum set pannalaam.

Quiz answer. Naalu pole motor, irupathi-anju hertz. Nooru-irubadhu into irupathi-anju, divided by naalu, sama ezhunooru-aimbadhu RPM. Answer B.
