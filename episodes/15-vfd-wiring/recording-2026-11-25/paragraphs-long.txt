Pudhu VFD panel-la fit pannaanga. Wiring ellaam neat-aa mudinjuchu. Main switch ON pannaanga. Rendu second-la, VFD ulla irundhu oru periya sattham, appuram pugai. Pudhu VFD, onnum odaama poiduchu. Thappu enga?

Electrician EB supply-ai VFD-oda U, V, W terminal-la connect pannirundhaaru. Motor-ai R, S, T-la. Input-um output-um maari pochu.

Veetla irukkura inverter-ai yosinga. Battery oru pakkam, veetu load innoru pakkam. EB supply-ai inverter output-la kuduthaa, inverter erinjidum. VFD-um adhey maadhiri dhaan. Endha pakkam supply varanum, endha pakkam motor pogumnu fix-aa irukku.

R, S, T pakkam ulla irukkuradhu rectifier diodes. Adhu AC-ai vaangi DC-aa maathum. U, V, W pakkam irukkuradhu IGBT switches. Avanga velai, supply kudukkuradhu, vaangaradhu illa. EB-oda four-fifteen volt-ai direct-aa IGBT mela kuduthaa, IGBT switch aagura moment-la supply short aagudhu. IGBT blast.

Adhanaala VFD power terminals-ai olunga theriyanum. R, S, T, idhula L1, L2, L3-nu-um irukkum. Idhu input, EB supply inga mattum dhaan. U, V, W, idhula T1, T2, T3-nu-um irukkum. Idhu output, motor inga mattum dhaan. PE, earth terminal. VFD-kkum motor-kkum earth kandippaa venum.

Chinna VFD-la single phase input-um irukkum. Appo L, N rendu terminal dhaan irukkum. Output mattum moonu phase, U, V, W.

Innum rendu terminal irukkum, P plus, N minus. Idhu ulla irukkura DC bus. Sila VFD-la B1, B2-nu brake resistor terminal irukkum. Ivanga-la supply-o motor-o kandippaa poda koodaadhu.

Ippo control terminals. Ivanga dhaan VFD-ku order kudukkuradhu.

Digital inputs. FWD, REV, multi speed. Idha common terminal-oda short pannaa, VFD run aagum. Oru push button-o, PLC output-o, inga connect pannalaam.

Analog input. Speed-ai potentiometer vechu maatha, moonu wire. Oru pakkam plus ten volt, innoru pakkam GND, naduvula irukkura wiper AI terminal-ku. Zero-la irundhu ten volt varaikkum maathinaa, speed zero-la irundhu full varaikkum maarum. PLC-la irundhu four-to-twenty milliamp-um kudukkalaam.

Relay output. VFD fault aanaa indha contact maarum. Adha panel-la alarm light-ku-o, PLC-ku-o kudukkalaam.

Mudhal mistake, VFD-kkum motor-kkum naduvula contactor-ai, motor odumbodhe OFF pannradhu. IGBT current kudukkitte irukkumbodhu, sudden-aa circuit open aagi, voltage spike varum. Rendaavadhu mistake, output-la power factor capacitor podradhu. VFD PWM pulses capacitor-la periya current spike-aa maarum. Capacitor-um burst aagum, VFD-um trip aagum. Output-la motor mattum dhaan.

Moonaavadhu mistake, motor-ai start, stop panna input main switch-ai adikkadi ON, OFF pannradhu. Ovvoru vaatiyum DC bus capacitor charge aagum, adhu VFD-ku stress. Run, stop-ai control terminal vazhiya dhaan pannanum.

Naangaavadhu mistake, control wire-ai power cable-oda orey tray-la kondu poradhu. Motor cable-la irundhu noise vandhu, speed signal aadum, sila neram VFD thappaa run, stop aagum. Control-ku shielded cable, power cable-la irundhu thalli.
